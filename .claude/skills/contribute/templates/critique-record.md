# Critique record format

The last section of every proposal and interpretation file. One line per question you asked, then the contributor's reply in their own words, indented under it. Write your questions as you asked them; copy their replies exactly. Never put suggested wording here.

```
- Q (Claude): You wrote "<their sentence>". <your question>
  A (<contributor name>): <their reply, as typed>
```

If they changed the draft after a question, their reply says so in their words (for example "I've added that"). The changed section itself is in their words too.
